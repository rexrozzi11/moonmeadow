Our Story

A Kitchen Full of Moonlight

Handmade, with heart

Moon & Meadow Bakery started a few months ago as a home kitchen project, and has grown into a little corner of Port Wentworth known for custom cakes made to order.

Every cake begins with a conversation — your flavors, your colors, your occasion — and ends with something that feels like it was baked just for you, because it was.

Policies & Ordering

Custom Cakes, Made to Order

Here's everything to know before placing an order — and what to include so nothing gets missed.

Ordering & Availability

  • Please allow at least 72 hours notice for most orders
  • Larger or custom cakes may need 1–2 weeks notice
  • Limited availability each week to ensure quality

Deposits & Payment

  • A 50% non-refundable deposit secures your order date
  • Remaining balance is due at pickup
  • Orders aren't confirmed until the deposit is received

Rush Orders

  • Last-minute orders considered based on availability
  • Rush orders may include an additional fee

Custom Cakes

  • Prices vary by size, design complexity & details
  • Inspiration photos are welcome, though designs may not be copied exactly
  • Minor artistic changes may be made to fit style

Pickup

  • Pickup in Port Wentworth, GA
  • Please arrive within your scheduled pickup window

Allergy Notice

  • Baked in a home kitchen
  • May process milk, eggs, wheat, soy, peanuts & tree nuts

How to Order

Please include the following details when placing your order:

  • Date needed
  • Theme / colors or inspiration photos
  • Flavor choice
  • Size / servings needed
  • Any special requests
